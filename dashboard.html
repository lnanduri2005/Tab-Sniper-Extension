Super bans

Blocked 24/7, even when Tab Sniper is off. Removing one takes a random challenge plus a cool-off.

    Watched sites

    Allowed, but cut off when you doomscroll: too long in one visit, too long on Shorts/Reels/feeds, or too many swipes. Warns at 80%, then a cooldown that doubles with each trip today.

      Bans

      Blocked while Tab Sniper is on or a focus session runs. Removable when it's off.

        Settings
